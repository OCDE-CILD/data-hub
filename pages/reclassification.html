English Learners: Reclassification Rate

About this Dashboard

This dashboard provides an interactive view of the reclassification rate data across Orange County schools and districts. Users can view 3 different versions of the dashboard to explore reclassification trends at various levels. Users can use the clickable tabs at the top of the dashboard to view either County, District or School-level data. Please note that there was a gap in CDE's public reporting of this data bewteen 202-21 to 2024-25. The data for 2021-22 and 2022-23 is not available.

When viewing and interacting with the visualization on a laptop screen, we recommend clicking the "see this in full screen" button in the bottom right under the visualization.

Key Terms
Reclassification Reclassification (formally, Reclassification Fluent English Proficient, or RFEP) is the process by which an English Learner student is determined to have developed sufficient English proficiency to be reclassified as fluent and exited from EL services and monitoring.
Reclassification Criteria California requires districts to consider four criteria when determining whether a student is ready to reclassify:
1. Assessment of English language proficiency — an objective assessment, such as the student's ELPAC Overall performance level.
2. Teacher evaluation — the student's classroom teacher(s) assessment of the student's academic performance.
3. Parent/guardian opinion and consultation — input from the student's parent(s) or guardian(s).
4. Comparison of basic skills — the student's performance on a measure of basic skills (such as an ELA assessment) compared to that of English-proficient students of the same age.
Reclassification Rate The percentage of English Learner students who were reclassified as Fluent English Proficient during a given year, out of the total EL Enrollment (see EL Count vs. EL Enrollment on the ELPAC dashboard for more detail on this denominator).

Methodology & Notes

Data is sourced from the California Department of Education, but has been edited by OCDE's REDI team to prepare the data file for data visualization and analysis. Small student group sizes may be suppressed in accordance with privacy guidelines. All rates are calculated using the data file's respective cumulative enrollment number as the denominator unless otherwise noted. For questions or more information about the methodology, please reach out to REDI@ocde.us
