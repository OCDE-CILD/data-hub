Suspension

About this Dashboard

This dashboard provides an interactive view of student suspension outcomes across Orange County schools and districts. Users can filter by district and school to examine suspension rates, suspended student counts, year-over-year trends, and outcomes across student groups. By highlighting patterns and disparities in exclusionary discipline, the dashboard helps educators identify student populations that may require additional supports, evaluate the impact of discipline practices over time, and inform strategies to promote equitable, inclusive learning environments.

When viewing and interacting with the visualization on a laptop screen, we recommend clicking the "see this in full screen" button in the bottom right under the visualization.

Key Terms
Suspension A suspension is the removal of a student from regular classroom instruction for disciplinary reasons, for one or more school days, as authorized under California Education Code. State reporting is based on out-of-school suspensions or instances where a student was removed from school entirely — rather than in-school disciplinary actions that keep the student on campus.
Suspension Rate The percentage of enrolled students who received one or more suspensions during the school year. This is an unduplicated count, a student suspended multiple times in the same year is only counted once, divided by cumulative enrollment.
Unduplicated Pupil Count (UPC) The Unduplicated Pupil Count reflects the number of students who are English Learners, eligible for Free or Reduced-Price Meals (socioeconomically disadvantaged), and/or in Foster Youth, counted only once per student even if they qualify under more than one category. UPC is used statewide for Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) purposes and is often reported alongside other student group breakdowns to give context on a school or district's overall student population.
Data Suppression To protect student privacy, results for any student group with fewer than 11 students are not reported. A group may appear at the district level but disappear when filtering to a specific school or year if it falls below this threshold — this does not mean there is no data, only that it can't be shown.

Methodology & Notes

Data is sourced from the California Department of Education, but has been edited by OCDE's REDI team to prepare the data file for data visualization and analysis. Small student group sizes may be suppressed in accordance with privacy guidelines. All rates are calculated using the data file's respective cumulative enrollment number as the denominator unless otherwise noted. For questions or more information about the methodology, please reach out to REDI@ocde.us
