About the OCDE Data Hub

The OCDE Data Hub is a centralized resource developed by the Orange County Department of Education to make student outcome data accessible, transparent, and actionable for educators, administrators, and community stakeholders across Orange County. The Hub brings together data from more than 25 school districts serving over 422,000 students, offering a consistent and comparable view of how students are performing countywide.

Our Purpose

The Data Hub exists to support data-informed decision making at every level — from district leadership to school-site teams. By surfacing key indicators like chronic absenteeism, graduation rates, college and career preparedness, English learner progress, and academic achievement, the Hub helps users understand where students are thriving and where additional support may be needed. All metrics are drawn from official California state data sources and are updated on a regular basis to reflect the most current available information.

What You'll Find Here

The Hub organizes its content into focused dashboard areas: Attendance & Engagement, College & Career Preparedness, Student Academics, English Learners, and Differentiated Assistance. Each dashboard allows users to explore data by student group, school, and over time. The Data Spotlight feature highlights emerging trends and notable findings across these areas, offering brief, accessible summaries for users who want quick insight without navigating full dashboards.

A Note on the Data

All data presented in the OCDE Data Hub is intended for informational and planning purposes. Metrics reflect aggregate, publicly reportable information and are consistent with California Department of Education reporting standards. Where small student group sizes may affect reliability, data is suppressed in accordance with privacy and reporting guidelines.
