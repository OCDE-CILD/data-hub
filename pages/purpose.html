About the OCDE Data Hub

Our Purpose

The OCDE Data Hub is a centralized resource developed by the Orange County Department of Education to make student outcome data accessible, transparent, and actionable for educators, administrators, families and community stakeholders across Orange County. The Hub brings together data from 29 school districts serving over 422,000 students, offering a consistent and comparable view of how students are performing countywide.

The Data Hub exists to support data-informed decision making. By surfacing key indicators like chronic absenteeism, graduation rates, college and career preparedness, English learner progress, and academic achievement, the Data Hub helps people across the county understand where students are thriving and where additional support may be needed. All metrics are drawn from official California Department of Education (CDE) data sources and are updated on an annual basis to reflect the most current available public information. Importantly, the data seen on this website is not provided by any school, district or charter across the county and uses only the publicly available data provided by CDE. If you are looking for real time data, please contact an the individual Local Education Agency whose data who are interested in.

What You'll Find Here

The Hub organizes its content into focused dashboard areas: Attendance & Engagement, College & Career Preparedness, Student Academics, English Learners, and Differentiated Assistance. Each dashboard allows users to explore data by student group, school, and over time. The Attendance & Engagement and Student Academics dashboard areas are both broken down into 3 sub-areas where. Attendance & Engagement has the following areas: Suspension, Graduation and Dropout Rate and Chronic Absenteeism. Student Academics has the following areas: Math Assessment, English Langauge Arts Assessment and Science Assessment. Finally, the Data Spotlight features an interesting finding, trend or idea that the Research, Evaluation & Data Inquiry (REDI) team at OCDE found worthwhile to highlight. The spotlight is updated several times per year.

A Note on the Data

All data presented in the OCDE Data Hub is intended for informational and planning purposes. Metrics reflect aggregate, publicly reportable information and are consistent with California Department of Education reporting standards. Where small student group sizes may affect reliability, data is suppressed in accordance with privacy and reporting guidelines. CDE releases these data on an annual basis and therefore, the data presented on this website showcases a point-in-time estimate. As a result of this, the data is from the previous academic year and therefore should not be used to make generalizations, or conclusions about current practices by any LEA.
