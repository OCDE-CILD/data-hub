About this Dashboard

This dashboard provides an overview of the California School Dashboard, a central system for accountability in the state. For more information on the California School Dashboard, you can visit: https://www.caschooldashboard.org/about/accountability/ To utilize this dashboard, use the filters on the right to explore data by district, school, student group, and year. To view data for an entire district, select "Overall District" under the "Select a School" filter.

Methodology & Notes

Data is sourced from the California Department of Education, but has been edited by OCDE's REDI team to prepare the data file for data visualization and analysis. For questions or more information about the methodology, please reach out to REDI@ocde.us
