Frequently Asked Questions

What is the OCDE Data Hub?

The OCDE Data Hub is an interactive resource developed by the Orange County Department of Education to provide accessible, transparent data on student outcomes across Orange County. It brings together key indicators from more than 29 school districts into a single, consistent platform for educators, administrators, families and community stakeholders.

Who is this site intended for?

The Data Hub is designed for those who are interested in educational outcomes of Orange County students, but may not have the technical background to navigate the California Department of Education downloadable files website. Those who work within schools and districts may find this resource useful, but ultimately out of date since the data provided by CDE is a year delayed. If an educator is interested in data visualization, and consolidation seen on this site, but using more up-to-date real time data please use the "Contact Us" button at the top right of the screen.

Where does the data come from?

All data is sourced from the California Department of Education and reflects official state reporting. It has been prepared and structured by OCDE's Research, Evaluation, and Data Insights (REDI) team for use in visualization and analysis. Data is updated on an annual basis as new state releases become available.

Why is some data missing or suppressed?

In accordance with California Department of Education privacy guidelines, data for student groups with fewer than 11 students is suppressed to protect individual privacy. This means some cells in tables or charts may appear blank or be marked as unavailable. This is intentional and required by state reporting standards.

Can I download the data?

Yes. Each dashboard page includes a link to a webpage where you can download the underlying data file used to build that dashboard. Files are provided in a txt format by CDE. If you need additional support to download or prepare data for use, please use the "Contact Us" button at the top right of the screen.

How do I interpret the metrics shown?

Each dashboard includes an About this Dashboard section that explains what the data represents and how to use the filters and views available. The Methodology & Notes section on each dashboard page provides additional context on how rates are calculated and any important caveats to keep in mind when interpreting results.

Who do I contact if I have questions or find an issue?

For questions about the data, a specific dashboard, or to report a technical issue, please reach out to OCDE's Research, Evaluation, and Data Insights team using the Contact Us link in the navigation menu. We welcome feedback and are committed to continuously improving the Hub.
