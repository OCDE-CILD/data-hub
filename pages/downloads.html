Original Data Sources

The dashboards on the OCDE Data Hub are built from data files published by the California Department of Education and other official sources. This page links directly to those original source files, and also provides the uncleaned CSV and excel sheets from the most recent year of data so you can download the data directly. Importantly, the CSV and excel sheets available for download here will not produce the same data as seen in the dashboards since the REDI team applies numerous data cleaning business rules to prepare the data for visualization. For more information on these data cleaning steps, see the Methodology and Notes page.
