Attendance & Engagement: Chronic Absenteeism

About this Dashboard

This dashboard provides an interactive view of chronic absenteeism across Orange County schools and districts. Users can filter by district and school to explore chronic absenteeism rates, student counts, year-over-year trends, and outcomes across student groups. By identifying patterns in student attendance and disparities among student populations, the dashboard helps educators target attendance interventions, monitor progress over time, and support strategies that improve student engagement and academic success. For more information about the data, see this page: https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/ad/crabtop.asp

When viewing and interacting with the visualization on a laptop screen, we recommend clicking the "see this in full screen" button in the bottom right under the visualization.

Methodology & Notes

Data is sourced from the California Department of Education, but has been edited by OCDE's REDI team to prepare the data file for data visualization and analysis. Small student group sizes may be suppressed in accordance with privacy guidelines. All rates are calculated using the data file's respective cumulative enrollment number as the denominator unless otherwise noted. For questions or more information about the methodology, please reach out to REDI@ocde.us
