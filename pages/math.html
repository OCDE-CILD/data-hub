Student Academics: Math Assessment

About this Dashboard

This dashboard provides an interactive view of The California Assessment of Student Performance and Progress (CAASPP) Math assessment results across Orange County schools and districts. Users can view 3 different versions of the dashboard to explore Math percent met/exceeded rates, test participation rates, year-over-year trends, and outcomes across student groups. Users can use the clickable tabs at the top of the dashboard to view either County, District or School-level data. By identifying patterns in student performance and disparities among student populations, the dashboard helps educators target educational interventions, monitor progress over time, and support strategies that improve student academic success.

When viewing and interacting with the visualization on a laptop screen, we recommend clicking the "see this in full screen" button in the bottom right under the visualization.

Key Terms
Standard Not Met / Nearly Met / Met / Exceeded CAASPP results are reported using four performance levels. Not Met and Nearly Met reflect achievement below the expected level for the grade/subject. Met reflects the expected level of achievement, and Exceeded reflects achievement above the expected level.
% Met or Exceeded This figure combines the Standard Met and Standard Exceeded performance levels into a single rate. It does not include students in the Nearly Met or Not Met levels. This is the standard proficiency figure used in most state and federal reporting.
Grade 12 vs. Graduating Class Grade 12 reflects students enrolled in 12th grade during a given school year — a single-year snapshot. Graduating Class (cohort) follows one group of students from their first entry into 9th grade through graduation, transfer, or dropout. These use different populations and should not be compared directly.
Parent Education Level Reflects the highest level of schooling completed by a student's parent(s)/guardian(s), as self-reported at the time of testing: Not a High School Graduate, High School Graduate, Some College, College Graduate, Graduate School/Post-Graduate, or Declined to State. Totals may not sum to the full student count due to non-response.
Data Suppression To protect student privacy, results for any student group with fewer than 11 students at the selected level are not reported. A group may appear at the district level but disappear when filtering to a specific school or year if it falls below this threshold — this does not mean there is no data, only that it can't be shown.

Methodology & Notes

Data is sourced from the California Department of Education, but has been edited by OCDE's REDI team to prepare the data file for data visualization and analysis. Small student group sizes may be suppressed in accordance with privacy guidelines. All rates are calculated using the data file's respective cumulative enrollment number as the denominator unless otherwise noted. For questions or more information about the methodology, please reach out to REDI@ocde.us
